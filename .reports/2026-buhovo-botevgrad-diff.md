# Scoring policy diff — Ботевград + Бухово 2026

**Old policy:** sum of per-day CSV `points` per (rider, event). Old
position is the rank by old points within each (event, category).  
**New policy:** combined-time ranking with tier-based fallback. New
position is the rank from the combined-time + tier algorithm.

Points table (2-day events, max 40):

`40, 34, 30, 27, 24, 22, 20, 18, 16, 14, 12, 10, 8, 7, 6, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1`

Ranking for 2-day events:

1. Full finishers (`FIN` both days) — sorted by sum of `time_ms`.
2. Day-1-only finishers — sorted by day-1 `time_ms`, placed below every Tier 1 rider.
3. Day-2-only finishers — sorted by day-2 `time_ms`, placed below every Tier 2 rider.

Riders with no FIN at all get 0 points, no position.

Detailed deltas follow, grouped by race + category. Only riders whose
points actually changed are listed. The `Δ pos` column shows how the
rider moved (negative = climbed, positive = dropped). The `tier` column
shows 1/2/3; `—` for ineligible riders.
